Correct AI Usage

I think AI is helpful when students use it to understand their classes better.
It can explain confusing topics and make practice questions for studying.
Students should check its answers and follow their professor's rules.
AI should help students build their skills instead of doing all their work.

AI Response

I agree that explanations and practice questions can support learning.
However, even helpful uses may be restricted by a professor's rules.
One thing I would add is that students should try problems themselves first.
Explaining the answer in their own words can show whether they understand it.

Benefits of AI

  1. Explains difficult topics in simpler ways.
  2. Creates practice questions to help students study.

Who Benefits?

  1. Students who need extra help understanding lessons.
  2. Employees who use approved AI tools to organize information.

How AI Tools Used

  • Getting feedback on a draft before revising it.
  • Brainstorming ideas before starting a project.

Incorrect AI Usage

Examples of Incorrect AI Usage

Submitting AI Work as Your Own
Copying an AI essay and turning it in as your own writing misrepresents who completed the assignment.

Using AI on a Restricted Test
Asking AI for test answers when the professor does not allow it breaks the rules of the class.

Creating Fake Sources
Using AI to invent quotes, research results, or sources puts false information into an assignment.

I think honesty should come first when students use AI. Turning in work that AI completed without permission can keep students from learning the material. Students are still responsible for checking what they submit, even when AI helped create it. Using AI responsibly means following class rules and being clear about the help received.

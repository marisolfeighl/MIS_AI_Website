Malware and Cyber Threats

The Jaguar Land Rover Cyberattack

On September 2, 2025, Jaguar Land Rover announced that it had been affected by a cyberattack. The company shut down its computer systems to limit the damage. This severely disrupted its vehicle production and retail activities. The company said it was working to restart its applications in a controlled way. To me, this incident shows that cybersecurity matters because a computer problem can interrupt real activities like building and selling cars.

Read Jaguar Land Rover's official statement about the incident.

Types of Malware

  1. Ransomware locks files and demands payment to restore access.
  2. Trojans pretend to be useful programs but contain harmful software.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Financial gain, such as stealing money or demanding ransom payments.
  2. Political activism, such as disrupting an organization to promote a cause.

Prevention Strategies

  • Use multifactor authentication to make stolen passwords harder to use.
  • Install software updates to fix security weaknesses that attackers could exploit.
